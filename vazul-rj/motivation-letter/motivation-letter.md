I am pleased to submit "vazul: An R Package for Analysis Blinding" for consideration in The R Journal.

Analysis blinding is a fundamental safeguard against bias, yet it has lacked a dedicated, standardized framework in the R ecosystem. Until now, researchers have relied on ad-hoc, custom scripts to create blind  datasets. This lack of standardization makes the process highly error-prone, limits reproducibility, and creates a significant barrier for researchers who are less technically proficient.

The vazul package fills this critical gap by providing a formal, high-level interface for masking and scrambling. By abstracting complex data manipulation into a user-friendly workflow, the package makes rigorous blinding accessible to a broader range of scientists. It ensures that blinding is no longer a technical hurdle but a standard, reproducible step in the research pipeline.

The package is available on CRAN, follows modern development standards, and is designed for long-term maintainability. We believe that the vazul package provides the necessary infrastructure to transition analysis blinding from a niche, custom practice into a transparent and approachable standard for the scientific community.

Thank you for your consideration.
